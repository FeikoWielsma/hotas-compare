The VPC VMAX Prime features a huge range of inputs including 7 total axis and 49 button inputs which can be further expanded with mode and shift functions. Designed to integrate into any VPCockpit or as a stand alone/desktop throttle unit - the VMAX Prime will take any cockpit to new heights. TOTAL INPUTS FUNCTION QTY Momentary Push Button 12 4-Way Hat (+ Push) 2 2-Way Hat (+ Push) 1 Contactless Analogue Stick (+ Push) 1 Contactless Dial Axis (+ Push) 1 Digital Axis Dial (+ Push) 1 Contactless Throttle Axis 2 Contactless Auxiliary Axis 1 Encoder Dial (+ Push) 2 Momentary Toggle Switches ([ON]-OFF-[ON]) 5 Mode Switch Dial 1 METAL CONSTRUCTION & THROTTLE HANDLES Premium metal construction including metal throttle grip handles with rubber pads - the VMAX Prime means business and will always ensure you have full control of your aircraft. NEW ERGONOMIC THROTTLE HANDLES Our all-new throttle handles are formed to the natural shape of your hands bringing incredible comfort whilst you're airborne. The button layout has been reworked from our previous throttle designs ensuring there is no crowding, minimising accidental inputs. ADJUSTABLE DETENT SYSTEM The VMAX Prime Throttle detents can be configured to require lifting to pass through a detent to prevent accidental crossover into the idle cutoff or afterburner range. Alternatively, push-through detent plates can be installed which provide tactile feedback when crossing the detent, without requiring the use of the detent-lifts. Included Detents: ★ Classic Plus - Lift to pass detents, push return but with an additional tactile bump immediately after lifting. ★ Warthog-Type - Lift to pass and lift to return detents. ★ Aerobatic - Push through and push return detents. ★ CosmoSim - Push through central detent. The detent plates can be adjusted forwards and backwards to position them where you need them within the axis range. The plates can also be mixed and matched between sets to provide a huge number of configuration possibilities. CONTACTLESS ANALOGUE STICK The analogue thumbstick has been upgraded to a contactless digital module. Ensuring immense precision and zero stick-drift! SWAPPABLE INPUT MODULES Customise your inputs to match your needs - the pre-installed hats and thumb button can be swapped to 4-Way + Push hats. ECO-LEATHER COVER Includes a premium eco-leather Expert Series cover to keep your pride and joy dust free!
