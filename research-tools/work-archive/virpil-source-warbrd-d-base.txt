Now incorporating independently adjustable axis clutch dampers which were first introduced on our flagship VPC MongoosT-50CM3 Flightstick Base, the WarBRD-D Base now also benefits from the same tunability. The clutches can be adjusted to suit a wide range of applications - from completely disengaged for fast, raw inputs, to semi-damped to prevent centering-oscillations and assisting steady aim, to fully locked to keep the stick from returning to the center. For convenience, the top cover allows direct access to the clutches, allowing on-the-fly fine tuning. With the new WarBRD-D Desk Mount Adapter retaining direct access to the clutch adjustments when installed on a VPC Desk Mount. The VPC WarBRD is the original dual-cam flightstick base. Offering unparalleled accuracy with precise and symmetrical axis forces, you'll never miss lining up a shot again. The WarBRD-D has also been updated with the detachable USB and AUX connectors, for reliable, convenient connection to your PC and the ability to link to other VPC devices for combined controllers to save on USB ports. Swappable axis springs and cams remain an important element of the available adjustments, allowing the pilot to change the centering force (springs), or centering and deflection feel (cams) as desired, but unlike the previous iteration of the VPC WarBRD - all cam sets are now included as standard! The VPC WarBRD-D Flightstick Base is a highly versatile base suited to a wide range of cockpits and flight styles! ADJUSTABLE CAM MECHANISM CAMS SPRINGS CLUTCH DAMPERS AviaSim Soft Center (Pre-Installed) The soft-center detent provides a suble tactile response when crossing the center point allowing it to be more easily located. Standard Springs (Pre-Installed) Reduced centering spring tension allowing for faster and less fatiguing inputs. Adjust the damping applied to each axis - pilots can fine tune the level of rebound, or even have the axis stay in place without a return to center, to suit their preferences. AviaSim No Center The no-center detent removes the tactile response when crossing the center point allowing for ultra precise and smooth inputs around the center point. Heavy Springs Provides a strong centering tension for increased feel and realism. CosmoSim Soft Center The soft-center detent provides a suble tactile response when crossing the center point allowing it to be more easily located. Extra Heavy Springs Extra strong centering tension for use with heavier metal grips and/or extensions for better centering response. CosmoSim No Center The no-center detent removes the tactile response when crossing the center point allowing for ultra precise and smooth inputs around the center point. CAM TYPES AVIASIM CAMS The "AviaSim" cams are tuned for atmospheric flight simulators. The centering force increases non-linearly the further away from the center the stick is. This lends to developing muscle memory for flying your aircraft on-the-end. COSMOSIM CAMS The "CosmoSim" cams are tuned for space sim pilots. The centering force increases linearly from the center point. This allows for fast wall-to-wall stick movements with ease when dogfighting. Each axis can be individually tuned with different springs and cams to create the axis response you want. A common configuration is using no-center cams on the pitch axis and soft-center cams on the roll axis to aid with finding level flight during disorientating maneuvers.
