These are the perfect addition to any VPCockpit to dramatically increase the number of programmable inputs and control you have over your aircraft. The Control Panel #2 brings even further unique inputs and control options to every cockpit, as a standalone device or as part of a VPCockpit. The full feature set includes a huge number of controls and configuration possibilities that will surely satisfy even the most avid of virtual pilots! The multi-position landing gear lever features an all metal internal mechanism, featuring a swappable detent plate, spring loaded lift and return and a VPC digital contactless position sensor ensuring precise and reliable input. The VPC Control Panel #2 features RGB momentary push buttons with a transparent cap which can be removed to inset your own button inlays - perfect for labelling your controls! Sticker inlay sheets are included with the VPC Control Panel to assist with labelling your buttons functions. The VPC Control Panel #2 can be added to your VPCockpit in a number of different ways. It can be used on a desktop using the included legs, it can be mounted onto the VPC Desk Mounts and it can also be combined with another Control Panel or to the VPC MongoosT-50CM2 Throttle to form a single unit. VPC Control Panels can operate as a standalone USB device, or they can be joined using the AUX connector to appear as a single input device which allows further control programming options such as controller the VPC Control Panel using the mode-dial on a joined throttle unit! Premium Construction Featuring premium manufacturing techniques and materials, the VIRPIL Controls Universal Control Panels are a marvel both inside and out! Spring-Loaded Landing Gear Mechanism The landing gear lever features a spring-loaded lift-to-move mechanism, preventing accidental inputs and more closely mimicing the input found on real aircraft. Swappable Detent Plate A swappable detent plate allows the landing gear lever to operate in a 2-position (UP-DOWN) mode, or a 3-position (UP-OFF-DOWN) mode to suit your preferences and chosen aircraft.
