The CDT-AEROMAX-RZ Flightstick sets a new standard for flightsticks by making high-end features more accessible than ever! The AEROMAX is built around it's revolutionary metal, dual-cam per axis gimbal. This is a significant departure from the plastic/pincer style gimbals found in this segment, allowing more pilots than ever to experience the immense precision usually only reserved for top-tier controllers. Importantly, the AEROMAX Flightstick is ultra compact, allowing pilots who fly with their controls directly on the desktop to fly without any ergonomic issues! Keep reading to find out what makes the CDT-AEROMAX Flightstick special! RIGHT HAND OMNITHROTTLE VARIANT OMNITHROTTLE PERFECT FOR SPACE PILOTS With a built in Z-extension, the CDT-AEROMAX-RZ is a perfect for space sim pilots who want a matching Z setup! METAL DUAL-CAM MECHANISM CNC METAL CONSTRUCTION Built to the highest standards, the gimbal is constructed with precision machined metal components resulting in extreme precision and axis stability. The CDT-AEROMAX puts metal where it counts! DUAL-CAM PER AXIS Dual symmetrical cams provide a compact centering mechanism, whilst ensuring smooth and even axis forces in all directions. CLUTCH DAMPERS With independant adjustable clutch axis dampers, you can fine tune the axis response on your CDT-AEROMAX Flightstick. HIGH QUALITY BEARINGS High quality bearings guarantee smooth axis response. VPC CONTACTLESS DIGITAL SENSORS Equipped with the same high precision VPC Contactless Digital Sensors found in our WarBRD-D and MongoosT-50CM3 Flightstick bases, the CDT-AEROMAX is never going to let you down! VERSATILE GRIP CONTROL SET HUGE CONTROL SET FOR ANY AIRCRAFT VERSATILE AEROMAX FLIGHT GRIP No matter what you fly, the AEROMAX Flightstick Grip features a huge control set to satisfy even the most demanding pilots! Attach the CDT Throttle Module to your AEROMAX-Z flightstick for additional axes*! ADJUSTABLE GRIP OFFSET INFINITELY ADJUSTABLE GRIP OFFSET BRINGING COMFORT TO DESKTOP FLIGHTSTICKS Our designers noticed that adjustable grip offset is sorely missing from this segment - and there is no reason it should be! The CDT-AEROMAX Flightstick allows you to adjust the grip offset to suit if you're flying center stick, side stick or anything in between with an easily adjustable grip offset! ULTRA COMPACT BASE ULTRA COMPACT BASE PERFECT FOR DESKTOP PILOTS The CDT-AEROMAX Flightstick is the worlds smallest dual-cam flightstick - bringing high-end precision right to your desktop! PREMIUM CONSTRUCTION A NEW ERA OF DESKTOP FLIGHTSTICKS HIGH QUALITY CONSTRUCTION Built to the same high standards VIRPIL Controls is known for, the Cadet Series and AEROMAX-R Flightstick brings ultimate quality, precision and versatile feature set to new and pro pilots on a budget! COMPREHENSIVE CONFIGURATION SOFTWARE The VPC Configuration Suite is a powerful collection of tools that offer a huge range of programmable and configurable options to take your setup even further. With the ability to configure axis-to-buttons, virtual axis, shift states, toggles and more. READY TO TAKE-OFF The VPC CDT-AEROMAX-RZ Flightstick is recognised as a standard USB input device without requiring additional drivers, and as such is directly compatible with a huge array of USB controller compatible games and flight sims.
