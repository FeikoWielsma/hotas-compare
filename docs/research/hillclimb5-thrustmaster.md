# Thrustmaster hillclimb5 — physical controls priority

Checked 2026-10-20. This pass follows the catalog priority: establish physical button counts, hat ways/push, axis counts, and whether controls are push, toggle, rotary/encoder, or analog. Weight, dimensions, and price were out of scope. Existing PDFs were rendered at 220 dpi with PyMuPDF under `work/hillclimb3-tm/` and `work/hc4-tm/` and read visually; where official documentation only publishes a physical-control aggregate, it is recorded as an aggregate without guessing button mechanisms.

## Resolved records

- **T.Flight HOTAS 4:** added 12 action buttons; official product page also states five axes, one trigger and one hat. [Official product page](https://www.thrustmaster.com/pt-br/products/t-flight-hotas-4/); [manual](https://ts.thrustmaster.com/download/accessories/Manuals/TFH4/T-Flight_Hotas_4_Manual.pdf). The manual's technical drawing names the action-button group but does not break down push versus switch or hat ways. Counts are physical group totals; mechanism classification remains partial.
- **T.Flight HOTAS X:** its official PC automatic-preset diagram maps 12 action inputs; three separate controls are preset, mapping and home. Existing record already contains those controls and five axes. Hat-way count is not stated. [Manual](https://ts.thrustmaster.com/download/accessories/Manuals/TFHX/t-flight_hotas_X_manual.pdf), diagram p.5.
- **T.Flight HOTAS One (original):** added the official 14-action-button total, five axes, one trigger and multidirectional hat. The source does not classify the 14 button bodies by mechanism or state hat-way/push behavior. [Official eShop](https://eshop.thrustmaster.com/en_us/t-flight-hotas-one.html); [manual](https://ts.thrustmaster.com/download/accessories/Manuals/TFHOne/T-Flight_HO_Manual.pdf).
- **T.Flight HOTAS One MSFS:** added 14 buttons alongside the named trigger and hat; official page explicitly gives five axes and dual rudder configuration. Button mechanism and hat ways remain unspecified. [Official product page](https://www.thrustmaster.com/en-us/products/t-flight-hotas-one-microsoft-flight-simulator-edition/).
- **TCA SKY Yoke Xbox/PC and PlayStation/PC:** added 24 physical action buttons to each edition; existing controls capture eight-way POV hat, ministick, two analog triggers, analog throttle, trim wheel and seven axes. The 24-button count is explicit on both official edition pages, but they do not classify the 24 mechanisms or state hat push/trim encoder behavior. [Xbox/PC page](https://www.thrustmaster.com/en-ie/products/tca-sky-yoke-xbox-pc/); [PlayStation/PC page](https://www.thrustmaster.com/products/tca-sky-yoke-playstation-pc/).
- **F/A-18C Hornet Add-On Grip:** filled the physical layout from the official eShop: one 8-way POV hat, two 4-way hats with push, a two-way control with push, four additional push buttons (including two pinky buttons), and a two-stage trigger. The advertised 19 action-button figure is mapped actions and is not represented as 19 physical buttons. No axes are published for the passive grip. [Official eShop control description](https://eshop.thrustmaster.com/en_us/f-a-18c-hornet-hotas-add-on-grip.html).
- **AIRBUS Add-On Grip:** retained official aggregate of five buttons, one dual-mode trigger, ministick and four axes. Official source does not number the control bodies or identify the five button mechanisms; trigger may be separately counted from the five buttons per wording. [Official eShop](https://eshop.thrustmaster.com/airbus-add-on-grip.html).
- **TCA Yoke Boeing:** verified the existing 18-button count against both console map and Windows Control Panel screenshot. The official manual screenshot shows buttons 1–18 on PC. [Manual](https://ts.thrustmaster.com/download/accessories/Manuals/TCA_Yoke_Boeing/TCA_Yoke_Boeing_Edition_user_manual.pdf), PC properties section and Xbox map p.7.
- **Warthog MKII Base / OMNI Extension:** base is a two-axis gimbal with no standalone button or hat bodies; OMNI is a mechanical extension with no electronic controls. The base's 128-button/8-axis capacity describes what the connected grip can expose, not controls mounted on the base. [MKII Base page](https://www.thrustmaster.com/nl-nl/products/hotas-warthog-mkii-base/); [OMNI page](https://www.thrustmaster.com/nl-nl/products/omni-extension/).

## Remaining ambiguous groups

These are kept partial and the uncertain mechanism/count fields remain unset rather than inferred from marketing totals or low-resolution angles:

- **T.Flight HOTAS 4:** aggregate 12 buttons is published, but individual push/switch split and hat ways are not. Product page/manual do not expose a numbered physical map.
- **Both NEO Ace Combat 8 editions:** official feature and mapping diagrams show hat/ministick/trigger/High-G button and throttle controls; many callouts combine multiple physical bodies, so no complete button-body inventory can be defended from the available guide diagrams. Do not transfer counts across the PS and Xbox editions.
- **Both TCA Sky Yokes:** exact 24 action-button bodies are documented, but push versus switch/rotary composition, mini-stick click, trim wheel mechanism and undisclosed axis names are absent from the official guides checked.
- **Quadrant Add-On Airbus:** official guide identifies 6 speed-brake and 4 flaps action inputs, 4 detents per lever and two axes. Diagram brackets combine wheel/knob/switch shapes; mechanism breakdown of the ten action controls is unresolved. [Manual](https://ts.thrustmaster.com/download/accessories/Manuals/TCA_Quadrant/TCA_Quadrant_Add-On_Airbus_Edition_user_manual.pdf), p.6.
- **Warthog Dual Throttle:** official page gives 17 throttle action-button events, a POV hat, two-axis slew control, two analog throttle levers, and a panel with 15 action-button events plus trim wheel; these action totals mix switches and pushes. The manual inventory records the positively identified push buttons and toggle bodies; remaining switch identities/return behavior remain partial. [Official page](https://www.thrustmaster.com/en-us/products/hotas-warthog-dual-throttle/); [manual/support page](https://support.thrustmaster.com/en/product/hotas-warthog-dual-throttles-en/).
- **Viper TQS:** Mode A map documents 19 mapped button inputs and five axes, including hat/push, stops and cut-off trigger. Input 17 and the mapping's split between analog axes and button contacts are not fully confirmed from the key; existing physical inventory remains partial. Keep the unresolved input 17 classification flagged. [Mission Pack manual](https://ts.thrustmaster.com/download/accessories/Manuals/Viper/Viper_Mission_Pack_User_Manual.pdf), p.8.
- **Viper Panel:** map documents 32 mapped button inputs and one trim-wheel axis; visually identifiable round/square push buttons and detent knobs were entered previously. Inputs 2–3, 17 and 21–32 cannot all be associated with a physical mechanism from the numbered key alone; leave their type unset. Same manual p.8.
- **Warthog Dual Throttle and Viper maps:** axis/button totals may refer to logical events or grouped switch positions, so no additional type-specific totals were fabricated beyond described physical bodies.
