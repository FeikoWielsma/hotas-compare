# Enthusiast catalog hillclimb 5 — remaining inventory audit

Checked 2026-10-20. Scope: VIRPIL, VKB, MOZA, and Slaw component and bundle records. The audit helper is `work/hotas_scope_audit.py`; it reports incomplete inventory evidence, unknown hat ways/push and optional control behavior, absent axis maps/counts, unknown platform entries, draft verification, and unresolved bundle component IDs. It deliberately does not edit catalog data.

## Targeted conclusions

- **MOZA MTP “27 Physical Switches” is supported by the exact MTP manual.** The MTP manual's Product Features section explicitly says “27 Physical Switches” ([manual](https://mozaracing.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/manual/MTP-Throttle-Panel-Manual-EN.pdf), p. 1). That is the MTP's own product claim, not an MH16 figure copied into the MTP record. The diagram does not itemize all 27 mechanisms or distinguish button, toggle, and selector types; retain the aggregate as `type: switch`, preserve `controls_evidence.coverage: partial`, and do not invent a breakdown. This is the reason the control type and coverage remain generic/partial.
- **VIRPIL Panel #3** is itemized from the exact titled product-page input list and its official mapping sheet. The four analog levers are X/Y/RX/RY; the mapping sheet confirms the 5-way dial center push ([product](https://virpil-controls.eu/vpc-universal-control-panel-3.html), [mapping](https://support.virpil.com/en/support/solutions/articles/47001264833-vpc-control-panel-3)). The seven ON-OFF-ON toggles' momentary behavior is not stated. The other three toggles are called both “latching” and `([ON]-OFF-[ON])`; preserve the inconsistency in notes and leave `momentary` unset. Ten tactile buttons and three separate pushbuttons remain distinct. Inventory coverage is complete.
- **VIRPIL Vector** base and gear MCP remain distinct: the base's two axes have documented pitch/roll movement; the gear lever's analog/discrete behavior is not stated on the [official gear MCP page](https://virpil-controls.eu/vector-gmc-panel.html). Leave that lever's analog property unset. This is a type detail gap, not an unknown physical control count.
- **VIRPIL ATLAS / ATLAS PRO** page statements cover the included emergency stop and two FFB axes, but do not settle total-versus-per-side throw or USB connector type. Keep those optional hardware properties unset as described in [ATLAS](https://virpil-controls.eu/atlas-ffb-base.html) and [ATLAS PRO](https://virpil-controls.eu/atlas-pro-ffb-base.html).
- **VIRPIL bundle lineup sweep:** the official store currently lists two bundle SKUs ([bundle listing](https://virpil-controls.eu/bundles)): WarBRD-D HOSAS Alpha Prime and WarBRD-D CADET HOTAS Alpha Prime. The HOSAS record already exists. Added `warbrd-d-cadet-alpha-prime-hotas.yaml` for the second SKU with its three component references from the official [bundle page](https://virpil-controls.eu/warbrd-d-cadet-hotas-bundle-alpha-prime-edition.html). The two included desk mounts/adapters are passive hardware and are outside the catalog component model. Existing custom Alpha Prime/WarBRD-D/CM3 combination is not presented as that official bundle.
- **MOZA bundles:** the AY210 yoke bundle and AB6 base/grip bundle are represented. The official AB9 page says its base is sold separately and must be paired with a compatible grip ([AB9](https://eu.mozaracing.com/products/ab9-base)); the earlier research found no exact official AB9+MH16 bundle SKU, so do not infer one. The FMP18 listing combines display/front-panel products that have no matching standalone component records; see [MOZA FMP18](https://eu.mozaracing.com/products/fmp18).
- **VKB verification:** the scoped audit found many records still marked `draft`. The existing VKB hillclimb notes trace several targeted inventory additions to official templates/manuals, but the pass did not independently establish every key field (including platform, hardware, and physical facts) for every draft record. Therefore no draft status was promoted solely because inventory is complete. `draft` is retained pending record-by-record source coverage.

## Audit interpretation and unresolved fields

The helper flags gaps in optional behavior fields as well as missing counts. A missing `ways` or `push` on a hat means the requested control inventory is not yet fully characterized; missing trigger action, switch momentary behavior, or button backlighting may be unreported optional behavior. `type: switch` is intentional where the official source documents a switch count but not a mechanism; do not force it into `button`, `toggle`, or `rotary` without evidence.

The audit output also reports unknown console/platform entries and draft verification for nearly every legacy record. The task priority was physical button counts, hat ways/push, axis counts/types, and resolvable references. These records were not mass-promoted or filled with inferred platform exclusions. Consult each component's `controls_evidence` and cited source before changing its status. Items remaining unset, with the evidence limitation and owning URL, include:

- VIRPIL B-JET rocker switch analog/push behavior; the [product page](https://virpil-controls.eu/b-jet-yoke-handle.html) lists the rockers but does not identify their electrical behavior.
- VIRPIL CDT-AERO and AEROMAX lever push states and slider position counts; the [AEROMAX mapping sheet](https://support.virpil.com/en/support/solutions/articles/47001275883-cdt-aeromax-r-flightstick) identifies axes but does not say that the analog levers are pressable or define discrete slider positions.
- VKB base rotary position counts where the official manual/template names a rotary but provides no detent count. A continuous or unnumbered rotary must not be assigned positions from appearance alone.
- MOZA MTP switch mechanisms, MTP ministick center push, and switch inventory breakdown; see the exact [MTP manual](https://mozaracing.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/manual/MTP-Throttle-Panel-Manual-EN.pdf).
- MOZA records whose inventory remains partial in the audit (AY210, MA3F EFCM/FCD, MA3X, MFY, MGX1000, MH16, MTP, MTQ, Z-axis module). Existing hillclimb notes explain the particular source limits and conflicts; do not promote these to complete until every physical input is reconciled to an exact product manual/template/diagram.
- Platform entries where a product's official page is silent. Silence is recorded as unknown, not unsupported. Passive Slaw hardware without a documented host platform also stays unknown.

The scoped audit found no component IDs in VIRPIL, VKB, MOZA, or Slaw bundles that fail to resolve to a same-brand component record. Script output includes only unresolved references, so none were emitted.
