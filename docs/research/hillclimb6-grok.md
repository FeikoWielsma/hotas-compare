## Stays unset

- **Quadrant Add-On speed-brake buttons.** Callout 3 brackets a thumbwheel and a toggle. Mapping numbers 21 and 22-26 do not land on separate bodies. The six stay an action-button aggregate. Virtual buttons 27-31 stay off the physical list. Coverage remains partial. Same add-on manual, features p.2 and mapping p.6.
- **Viper Panel remainder.** Landing-gear lever is named on features p.6, but the Mode A leaders for 42-53 (two-arrow control, gear-shaped knob, further round controls) could not be assigned without swapping endpoints between reads of the same drawing. Backlight of individual buttons is not stated; "Backlit VIPER PANEL" names the panel. Programmable LEDs are a display, not buttons.
- **T.Flight HOTAS 4, HOTAS X, HOTAS One, HOTAS One MSFS, both NEO editions.** English manuals name action-button groups, a multidirectional hat, throttle, twist, and a rudder rocker. They do not number hat ways or split the action-button total into push versus switch. NEO PS and Xbox manuals use different console-button callouts (PS CREATE/OPTIONS/SHARE versus Xbox VIEW/MENU/SHARE), so counts were not copied across editions. Coverage stays partial.
- **TCA SKY YOKE, both editions.** No control manual on the support pages checked in earlier rounds, and this pass did not find a numbered body diagram. The 24 action buttons, 8-way hat, ministick, two triggers, throttle and trim wheel stay as already recorded. Hat push, ministick click, and trim-wheel mechanism stay unset.
- **AIRBUS Add-On Grip.** The instruction sheet is assembly and driver links, not a control map. The product-page aggregate (5 buttons, dual-mode trigger, ministick, 4 axes) stays. Trigger stages and button mechanisms stay unset. [Sheet](https://ts.thrustmaster.com/download/accessories/manuals/Airbus_grip/AIRBUS_GRIP_Instruction_Manual.pdf).
- **Warthog Dual Throttle.** The manual's own grip and panel lists already itemize hats, the push button, and the 2- and 3-position switch groups. Which of the five panel 2-position switches are the two called permanent is still not identified, so momentary stays unset and coverage stays partial. [Manual](https://ts.thrustmaster.com/download/accessories/pc/hotas/manual/HOTAS_Warthog/HOTAS_Warthog_Manual.pdf), pp.2-3.
- **Hornet grip** inventory was already complete. Trigger action, the 8-way hat's push, and the 2-way control's momentary behavior are not stated on the product or eshop pages.
- **TCA Sidestick X prices.UK.** The UK product page sticky price is GBP 99.99 and the UK shop `product:price:amount` is GBP 89.99. Left unset. [UK product](https://www.thrustmaster.com/en-gb/products/tca-sidestick-x-airbus-edition/), [UK shop](https://shop.thrustmaster.com/en_gb/tca-sidestick-x-airbus-edition.html).
