# Logitech hardware unknowns — round 3

Checked 2026-10-20. Scope: Logitech component and bundle records only. I inspected the rendered product-tour pages in Logitech's own standard X52, X52 Pro, Flight Yoke System, and Flight Throttle Quadrant manuals. Manuals were downloaded with a browser user agent after plain requests were denied; page references below are the printed English page numbers.

## Changes made

- **Standard X52 stick:** Kept the existing `x52-space-flight-stick` content ID so bundle references remain valid; renamed its displayed product to “Logitech G X52 Flight Stick.” Replaced the Pro-manual evidence with the standard [X52 HOTAS user guide](https://www.logitech.com/assets/65328/2/x52-hotas.pdf). Its product tour (p. 3) labels a two-stage trigger, three backlit buttons on the stick head, two 8-way hats, a missile-launcher button, a 3-position mode selector, three spring-loaded base switches, and a pinkie switch. The three base switches remain three physical switches, not six buttons. The manual does not state that the hats are pressable, so `push` is omitted. The standard manual does not establish the stick sensor type, so the previous Hall and potentiometer claims were removed rather than carried over from X52 Pro.
- **Standard X52 throttle:** The standard guide's product tour (p. 4) separately confirms two throttle-head fire buttons, one thumb slider, two rotary axes, one 8-way POV hat, a mouse controller that has an alternate hat mode, a left mouse button, a clutch button, and a rear scroll wheel with a built-in button. Added the two buttons, replaced source links with the standard manual, and kept the inventory partial because the mouse controller's complete physical input behavior is not established. Removed its unsupported potentiometer assertion.
- **X52 standard bundle:** Set PC to supported from the standard X52 manual's Windows setup instructions. Xbox and PS5 remain unknown because the manual does not specify them. The component ID in the bundle remains unchanged.
- **X52 Pro records and bundle:** Round 2's visually verified manual pages (stick p. 3; throttle p. 4) establish three backlit stick-head fire buttons and two throttle-head fire buttons. The existing records already reflect those counts. PC is supported by the manual's Windows setup instructions; Xbox and PS5 remain unknown.
- **Flight Yoke System yoke:** Replaced the older Saitek blog PDF as control evidence with the current Logitech [Flight Yoke System user guide](https://www.logitech.com/assets/65128/flight-yoke-system.pdf). Page 4 visually locates one 8-way hat and one 2-way rocker on the left handle; two 2-way rockers, a 3-position mode selector, and a single-function button on the back of the right handle; and a single-function button on the back of the left handle. It also shows three chronograph buttons below the display (function, start/stop, reset). Preserved three physical rockers rather than treating six directional commands as six buttons. The exact system manual supports PC for the bundle; Xbox/PS5 are unknown.
- **Flight Throttle Quadrant:** Replaced the 2014 blog PDF with Logitech's [Flight Throttle Quadrant guide](https://www.logitech.com/assets/65155/2/flight-throttle-quadrant.pdf). Page 4 shows three smooth-travel levers with zero-button detents and three 2-way rocker switches. Replaced the erroneous six-button group with three physical rockers (six directions); the three bottom detent button actions remain represented with the levers/detents. The guide's Windows instructions establish PC support; Xbox/PS5 remain unknown.

## Checked, but left unknown

- **Extreme 3D Pro:** The official Logitech product page still gives a “12 programmable buttons” aggregate, an 8-way hat, a rapid-fire trigger, and twist control. Its aggregate does not establish standalone physical button count or whether the trigger is included. No official model-specific manual with an unambiguous physical inventory was found in Logitech search/support results, so the YAML remains partial and does not convert 12 programmable commands into button count. [Official product page](https://www.logitechg.com/en-us/shop/p/extreme-3d-pro-joystick).
- **Dimensions and mass:** The inspected model manuals and accessible official SKU pages do not provide verifiable dimensions or mass for the assigned X52, yoke, quadrant, rudder, or Extreme 3D Pro records. These values remain absent.
- **Materials and hardware construction:** The standard X52 guide does not identify its sensor technology or material composition. The X52 stick sensor/gimbal and X52 throttle sensor are left unknown. No new sensor, material, or gimbal details were inferred from another variant.
- **Console compatibility:** The Logitech manuals inspected document Windows/PC setup and use. They do not provide an exhaustive Xbox or PS5 statement. Console fields remain `unknown`; absence of console support evidence is not recorded as `unsupported`.
- **Yoke and pedals:** The Logitech yoke guide covers the yoke and included throttle quadrant. Rudder pedal product/manual information continues to establish three axes (rudder plus independent left/right toe brakes); it names no hand-operated buttons or switches. No unlisted controls were added.
