# Thrustmaster and MOZA catalog unknowns — round 1

Checked 2026-10-20. This round covers all 53 baseline records assigned to Thrustmaster (35) and MOZA (18), plus two exact Thrustmaster variants split out from the former combined HOTAS One / 4 / X record. Product claims below were taken from first-party product listings, official support pages, manuals, configuration guides, and official stores only. Existing research notes were read first: [MOZA controls rounds 1–2](controls-moza-1.md), [Thrustmaster controls rounds 1–2](controls-thrustmaster-1.md), [Thrustmaster follow-up 2a](controls-thrustmaster-2a-followup.md), [Thrustmaster follow-up 2b](controls-thrustmaster-2b-followup.md), and the [Thrustmaster catalog refresh](catalog-refresh-thrustmaster.md) / [Sol-R catalog refresh](catalog-refresh-solr.md). Previously encountered page or extraction dead ends were not repeated when an official alternative locale, product page, support page, or manual already supplied the evidence.

## Changes applied

- Added a sourced `platform_compatibility` object to all baseline component and bundle records in this group. Exact edition differences are retained: T.Flight HOTAS One and HOTAS X have distinct platform profiles, T.Flight HOTAS 4 is now a separate record, Neo PlayStation and Xbox editions stay separate, and the rudder pedal accessory documents the compatible console host requirement.
- Preserved the existing HOTAS One component path and references, but changed its name and evidence note to cover only HOTAS One. Added separate HOTAS 4 and HOTAS X component records. Aggregate action-button marketing totals are not expanded into made-up button types.
- Added or corrected product-specific physical control, mechanism, materials, dimensions, mass, sensor, axis totals, connectivity, and software facts where the official source was specific. MOZA support pages corrected AB6 encoder resolution to 15-bit, and state exact dimensions/materials/weight and PC-only compatibility for AY210, AB9, MRP, MTP and MTQ where published. MOZA's stated programmable-input totals remain separate from physical control counts.
- MOZA console fields remain `unknown` for products whose exact official source does not explicitly list console denial, including conditional grips and bases. Products whose individual official support pages say “PC only” are marked unsupported on consoles.
- Baseline gap accounting: **53 products, 752 recorded gaps before this round; 183 baseline fields resolved and 569 remain unresolved**. This is a baseline-gap comparison, not a claim that the catalog is complete. The 2 newly separated HOTAS variants were not part of baseline gap counts.

## Source-specific cautions

- **MOZA J04:** the official MFY page/manual does not identify a separate J04 retail product; the catalog record remains a provisional alias, with no invented J04-only specifications. The [MFY support page](https://support.mozaracing.com/en/support/solutions/articles/70000679589-moza-mfy-yoke-support) is the official matching source.
- **MOZA AY210 switches and MTQ totals:** AY210 support calls 13 controls “switches” and notes long-press/RGB but does not document switch positions/momentary mechanics. MTQ support advertises 23 total controls while also listing 10 multifunction buttons, three rotary knobs, 17 toggle switches and one gear lever; those figures conflict, so panel inventory remains partial and documented control subtypes are not inflated to make the sums agree. [AY210](https://support.mozaracing.com/en/support/solutions/articles/70000679586-moza-ay210-yoke-base-support), [MTQ](https://support.mozaracing.com/en/support/solutions/articles/70000679590-mtq-throttle-quadrant-support).
- **MOZA hardware:** AB9 support explicitly says PC only and ±20° adjustable pitch angle but does not provide an axis list, device mass, or dimensions in the accessible specification. It remains without fabricated axis labels. MRP documents 3 Hall-sensed physical pedal inputs, dimensions, mass, materials, adjustable end stops/centering and PC platform; its hardware `throw_deg` is left unset because the same mechanism has distinct rudder and toe-brake travel. [AB9](https://support.mozaracing.com/en/support/solutions/articles/70000668982-moza-ab9-base-support), [MRP](https://support.mozaracing.com/en/support/solutions/articles/70000684204-mrp-rudder-pedals).
- **Thrustmaster T.Flight One / 4 / X:** official sources distinguish the three variants. One is PC/Xbox, HOTAS 4 is PlayStation/PC, and X is PC/PS3. No single record now projects all three console profiles. Sources: [One manual](https://ts.thrustmaster.com/download/accessories/Manuals/TFHOne/T-Flight_HO_Manual.pdf), [HOTAS 4 manual](https://ts.thrustmaster.com/download/accessories/Manuals/TFH4/T-Flight_Hotas_4_Manual.pdf), [HOTAS X product page](https://www.thrustmaster.com/products/t-flight-hotas-x/).
- **TCA Boeing compatibility correction:** current exact pages list the TCA Yoke Boeing Edition, Quadrant Boeing Edition, and complete Yoke Pack for PC and Xbox. The quadrant requires connection to the yoke host on Xbox. This corrects the prior PC-only projection in the yoke and quadrant records and is applied to the complete bundle. [Yoke](https://www.thrustmaster.com/en-us/products/tca-yoke-boeing-edition/), [Quadrant](https://www.thrustmaster.com/en-us/products/tca-quadrant-boeing-edition/), [Xbox connection instructions](https://support.thrustmaster.com/en/kb/1772-en/), [complete pack](https://www.thrustmaster.com/en-us/products/tca-yoke-pack-boeing-edition/).
- **TFRP pedals:** console compatibility is conditional on a compatible Thrustmaster host joystick. The standalone USB/RJ12 accessory is not represented as a console controller by itself. [Official compatibility page](https://www.thrustmaster.com/en-gb/products/t-flight-rudder-pedals/), [official connection guide](https://support.thrustmaster.com/en/kb/1776-en/).
- Hats or switches are not assigned assumed push, direction, position, momentary, or backlighting behavior. A marketing total such as “programmable actions,” “signals,” or “physical controls” is not converted to a standalone push-button count. Inventory is marked complete only where a product-specific manual/diagram establishes all installed inputs.
