# VIRPIL catalog unknowns — round 3

Checked 2026-10-20. Scope is VIRPIL records only. Current YAML and the parent integration note were treated as the baseline.

## Applied

- Corrected the Hawk-60 grip's `hardware.sensor` to `null`. VIRPIL describes this as a detachable grip that connects to a compatible base, and its exact product page lists the grip inputs without claiming an onboard sensor. The base's sensor type must not be inherited by the grip. The record already correctly has no grip axes, no standalone USB, and VPC Configuration Tool software. [Hawk-60 product page](https://virpil-controls.eu/vpc-hawk-60-collective-grip.html), [Hawk-60 support manual](https://support.virpil.com/en/support/solutions/articles/47001193239-hawk-60-collective-grip-user-manual)
- Expanded the evidence notes for the four CDT-AEROMAX fixed-stick variants. The official CDT-AERO L/R listings say those grips use the same design introduced with AEROMAX and provide a physical control list. Their official one-page mapping templates were downloaded and visually inspected; the templates depict button outputs and rX/rY/rZ/SLDR axes. However, the templates show blank profile/revision fields and do not identify the AEROMAX fixed-stick SKUs, so all four records remain `coverage: partial` until VIRPIL publishes a revision-matched AEROMAX control list. The AEROMAX product pages already document VPC Configuration Software and standard USB input-device compatibility, represented in the records. [CDT-AERO L listing](https://virpil-controls.eu/cdt-aero-l-grip.html), [CDT-AERO R listing](https://virpil-controls.eu/cdt-aero-r-grip.html), [L mapping sheet](https://support.virpil.com/en/support/solutions/articles/47001285225-vpc-cdt-aero-l-grip), [R mapping sheet](https://support.virpil.com/en/support/solutions/articles/47001285226-vpc-cdt-aero-r-grip), [AEROMAX L listing](https://virpil-controls.eu/cdt-aeromax-l-flightstick.html), [AEROMAX R listing](https://virpil-controls.eu/cdt-aeromax-r-flightstick.html)

## Remaining limits

The store publishes these dimension triples: ACE `352×537×245 mm`, Panel #1 `236×202×83 mm`, Panel #2 `236×201×164 mm`, Hawk-60 `207×122×100 mm`, and Rotor Plus `369×209×122 mm`.
