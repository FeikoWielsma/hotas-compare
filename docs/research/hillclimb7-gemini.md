## 3. Gaps Left Unset and Rationale

The following items from `work/price-gaps-r7.txt` remain unset in accordance with catalog guidelines:

### Component Gaps
1. **`blackbox-mk2:UK`**: Not sold on `flightsimcontrols.co.uk`. The UK store only stocks the BlackBox Mk3 (product ID 1360).
2. **`f14-add-on-grip-gunfighter-mk-iii:UK`** & **`f14-add-on-grip-gunfighter-mk-iv:UK`**: Standalone Gunfighter F-14 grips are not listed in the UK store (only the Gladiator NXT EVO F-14 joystick is offered).
3. **`gnx-scg-premium-grip-with-ota-l:EU`**: Not sold as a standalone grip in the EU store (EU only lists the standalone OTA conversion kit or the complete Gladiator NXT EVO Omni-Throttle joystick).
4. **`gnx-scg-premium-grip-with-ota-r:EU`** & **`gnx-scg-premium-grip-with-ota-r:UK`**: Neither EU nor UK official stores list the right-hand standalone SCG grip with preinstalled OTA.
5. **`kg12-gen-2-add-on-grip-gunfighter-mk-iii:EU`** & **`:UK`**: Not sold as an active SKU. In the EU store, the Gen.2 standalone grip defaults to Rev.C (Gunfighter IV), with Rev.B only available via special order notes; absent entirely from the UK store.
6. **`kg12-gen-2-add-on-grip-gunfighter-mk-iv:UK`**: Standalone KG12 Gen.2 grips are not stocked on `flightsimcontrols.co.uk`.
7. **`kg12-gf-gen-1-add-on-grip-for-gunfighter-mk-iii:EU`** & **`:UK`**: Discontinued legacy Gen.1 grip is not listed in EU or UK stores.
8. **`mcg-pro-add-on-grip-gunfighter-mk-iii:EU`** & **`:UK`**: Discontinued Rev.B socket variant is not listed for sale.
9. **`mcg-pro-add-on-grip-gunfighter-mk-iv:UK`**: Standalone MCG Pro grips are not sold on `flightsimcontrols.co.uk`.
10. **`mcg-ultimate-add-on-grip-gunfighter-mk-iii:UK`**: Standalone MCG Ultimate grips are not sold on `flightsimcontrols.co.uk`.
11. **`scg-standard-grip-gunfighter-mk-iii-l:EU` / `:UK`**, **`scg-standard-grip-gunfighter-mk-iii-r:EU` / `:UK`**, **`scg-standard-grip-gunfighter-mk-iv-l:EU` / `:UK`**, **`scg-standard-grip-gunfighter-mk-iv-r:EU` / `:UK`**: The EU store only sells the **SCG Premium** standalone grip for Gunfighter (`VKBSIM-G-GF-SCG-L-P` and `VKBSIM-G-GF-SCG-R-P`), and the UK store sells no Gunfighter SCG grips. Under the strict configuration matching rule (no substitutions between Standard and Premium), all 8 SCG Standard Gunfighter regional entries remain unset.
12. **`stecs-modern-throttle-grip-premium-combo:UK`**: The UK store only stocks the Standard grip (`VKBSIM-A-STECS-MTG2-GRIP-STD`) and Upgrade Kit, but not the Premium Combo. Kept unset per configuration rules.
13. **`stecs-stg-grip-r:EU`** & **`:UK`**: Neither EU nor UK lists the Right-Hand standalone STG grip (only the Left-Hand add-on grip `VKBSIM-A-STECS-STG-L` is cataloged). Kept unset.
14. **`wwii-gnx-thq-add-on-grip:UK`**: Not stocked on `flightsimcontrols.co.uk`.
