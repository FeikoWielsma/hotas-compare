# WINCTRL catalog unknowns — round 3

Checked 2026-10-20. Scope: the 11 WINCTRL component YAML records and 3 WINCTRL bundle records listed below. No other brand records were edited.

## Source review and edits

I revisited WINCTRL's exact-SKU storefront pages, the official Download Center/manual links carried by the records, the official Orion 2 joystick-base and throttle-base pages, and the older official WINCTRL comparison/listing pages referenced by earlier research. The exact product pages for goods IDs 333, 341, 543, 551, 559, and 1065 return a JavaScript template rather than rendered product details in text retrieval. I opened ID 375 in the browser: the live page is titled **Orion2 NavyAce Throttle Combo**, priced €204.33, with a combo discount to €171.99, and lists an Orion2 throttle base, NavyAce metal throttle grip, and dual-throttle connector. It does not identify the assigned F/A-18 HOTAS bundle or F/A-18 throttle handle SKU. I corrected the evidence notes in `orion-2-f18-hotas.yaml` and `orion-2-f18-throttle.yaml` to record this source mismatch; I did not copy its price or hardware into the F/A-18 records. The official Orion 2 joystick base page is readable and describes its cam/spring modes, adjustable damping, extension slots, force-sensing accessory, and SimApp mapping/calibration. The current `orion-2-base.yaml` already carries these hardware facts, so no source-backed value was missing to add there. The only Orion base drawing found in the prior round is explicitly for J2-2, not confirmed as Orion 2; its dimensions were not applied.

The official legacy F-18 listing [goods ID 314](https://eu.winctrl.com/view/goods-details.html?id=314) contains a comparison of NavyAce/ViperAce variants. It confirms a subset of grip controls, but does not establish that the described NavyAce grip is the exact current standalone FA-18 grip SKU. Existing YAML keeps that caveat and does not claim a complete inventory. The exact ViperAce throttle grip 2.0 source [goods ID 1765](https://eu.winctrl.com/view/goods-details.html?id=1765) was reviewed in the prior pass; it supplies the represented controls, but not the remaining individual control behavior or physical measurements.

No new platform compatibility was recorded. WINCTRL's exact product and bundle sources reviewed here do not explicitly name Xbox or PS5 support or incompatibility. USB/computer operation does not prove console support or denial, so those fields remain `unknown`. No current regional checkout prices were captured for the blank bundle regions; regional/discount prices are time- and storefront-dependent and were not inferred from other SKUs.

No new technical specification values could be safely applied from this pass. Empty/unknown values remain where the exact model, revision, or property could not be established. This includes the Cyber Taurus model-dependent measurements, the F-16EX versus standard ViperAce control-map distinction, the PFP3/PFP-3N identity mismatch, and the J2-2 versus Orion 2 base identity mismatch. The only YAML edits were source-evidence corrections for the live ID 375 mismatch described above.
