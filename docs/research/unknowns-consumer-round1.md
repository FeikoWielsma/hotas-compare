# Consumer catalog unknowns — round 1

Checked 2026-10-20. This pass covered the 41 consumer-brand component and bundle records enumerated in `work/catalog-unknowns-before.json` for Honeycomb, Logitech, Turtle Beach, MFG and Slaw.

## Changes applied

- Added platform compatibility evidence to all 41 records. Honeycomb product records identify PC support; Alpha XPC lists Xbox One/Series support and the non-XPC Honeycomb pages list PC/Mac only. Logitech records identify PC support but leave console status unknown because this pass did not establish an exhaustive per-SKU console exclusion. Turtle Beach Flightstick / Flightstick II and yoke system are recorded as PC/Xbox capable based on their official pages; standalone throttle and pedal accessories leave console support unknown because console host support is not established on their accessory pages. MFG and Slaw pedals remain PC supported with console support unknown: these are independent USB devices and no official console host statement was found. Bundle compatibility remains unknown because exact complete-SKU platform support was not established. Sources: [Honeycomb XPC](https://flyhoneycomb.com/products/alpha-flight-controls-xpc), [Honeycomb product catalog](https://flyhoneycomb.com/collections/all), [Logitech G flight products](https://www.logitechg.com/en-us/products/flight.html), [Turtle Beach Flightstick](https://www.turtlebeach.com/products/velocity-one-flight-stick), [Turtle Beach Flightstick Xbox setup](https://support.turtlebeach.com/s/article/VelocityOne-Flightstick-Xbox-Setup?language=en_US), [MFG Crosswind V3](https://mfg-sim.com/rudders/mfg-crosswind-v3-base.html), [Slaw downloads/support](https://slawdevice.com/en/content/7-downloads).
- Corrected Alpha XPC sensor to Hall effect, and populated its sourced 180-degree travel, damped self-centering action, dimensions (273 × 410 × 208 mm), mass (4,650 g) and steel shaft. Its detailed per-handle diagram and broad prose disagree on the number/distribution of POV hats and buttons, so controls coverage is partial. The XPC page explicitly describes the upgraded Hall sensors and Xbox One / Series support. [Honeycomb Alpha XPC specifications](https://flyhoneycomb.com/products/alpha-flight-controls-xpc).
- Corrected the original Alpha sensor to unknown (`null`): the official original-Alpha product page describes a shaft and bearings but does not identify the sensor technology. Filled its sourced 180-degree travel, damped self-centering/no-center-detent behavior, 273 × 410 × 208 mm dimensions, 4,769 g mass and steel shaft. [Honeycomb original Alpha specifications](https://flyhoneycomb.com/products/alpha-flight-controls).
- Kept Turtle Beach Flightdeck's 39 programmable stick-module functions separate from physical controls. Existing partial inventory records only individually named levers/dials; manufacturer aggregate function and axis totals do not establish a complete physical button map. [Flightdeck product page](https://www.turtlebeach.com/products/velocity-one-flight-deck), [Hangar app control customization](https://support.turtlebeach.com/articles/en_US/Knowledge/VelocityOne-Flightdeck-Customize-Controls-With-VelocityOne-Hangar-App).

## Irreducible unknowns and source attempts

- **Honeycomb Alpha and XPC controls:** official product pages were checked for the current broad copy and detailed left/right callouts. Their descriptions conflict on hat/button counts and PTT placement; the five panel switch functions are named but do not fully specify the complete switch mechanism/layout. Do not treat function names as additional proven physical controls. [Alpha](https://flyhoneycomb.com/products/alpha-flight-controls), [XPC](https://flyhoneycomb.com/products/alpha-flight-controls-xpc).
- **Original Alpha sensor:** official product page checked; no sensor type is specified. Cleared prior magnetic/contactless assertion as unsupported.
- **Honeycomb non-Alpha inventory and mechanism details:** official product pages and manufacturer download/software pages are the available sources; see the linked prior Honeycomb research for product-by-product statements. Some marketing function totals, alternative lever/detent parts, and output LEDs do not resolve installed physical switch details. Leave those records partial where the installed input inventory is not fully enumerated. [Honeycomb downloads](https://flyhoneycomb.com/pages/downloads).
- **Logitech physical controls:** Logitech-hosted manuals and Logitech/Saitek product pages were checked, as listed in the prior Logitech research. Extreme 3D Pro's “12 programmable buttons” is not a physical count because the source does not say whether the trigger is included. X52 and X52 Pro variant/control maps do not resolve every switch action or backlighting detail. Logitech support/product pages do not establish console host compatibility for these USB devices. [Extreme 3D Pro](https://www.logitechg.com/en-us/shop/p/extreme-3d-pro-joystick), [X52 Pro manual](https://www.logitech.com/assets/65327/x52-professional-hotas.pdf), [X56 manual](https://www.logitech.com/assets/65324/x56-hotas.pdf).
- **Turtle Beach controls:** official Flightstick handbook, product pages and official support pages were checked. Flightstick Xbox navigation is described functionally, but the support image does not provide an unambiguous count of distinct physical navigation buttons. Flightdeck physical stick-module inventory remains partial because 39 programmable functions and 139 configurable assignments are aggregate mappings; screen cells are configurable touch UI, not fixed buttons. [Flightstick handbook](https://cdn.turtlebeach.com/device/qig/velocityone--flightstick/velocityone--flightstick_367-6703-v1.pdf), [Xbox navigation guide](https://support.turtlebeach.com/s/article/VelocityOne-Flightstick-Xbox-Setup?language=en_US), [Flightdeck](https://www.turtlebeach.com/products/velocity-one-flight-deck).
- **MFG and Slaw accessories / legacy products:** official MFG Crosswind pages and Slaw product/download pages were checked. Standalone accessory records do not specify whether they work when plugged into a console through a particular host; this stays unknown. The prior Viper V2 note documents that current V3 documentation cannot prove every V2 detail. No unsupported dimensions, mass, materials, or switch inventory was copied from a related generation or bundle. [MFG accessory listings](https://mfg-sim.com/rudder-acessories/), [Slaw Viper V2 notice](https://slawdevice.com/en/blog/post/7-Announcement-RX-Viper-V2-rudder-pedals.html), [Slaw V3 page](https://slawdevice.com/en/glowna/47-pre-order-rx-viper-v3-rudder-pedals-silver.html).
