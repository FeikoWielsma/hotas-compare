# Control inventory integration review

Checked 2026-10-19. This note records the parent review after the Luna research and correction rounds. Current YAML and [the catalog audit](../control-data-audit.md) are authoritative; earlier notes preserve the research trail and may describe limitations subsequently removed.

## Corrections made during integration

- TWCS: visually inspected the actual [manufacturer mapping diagram](https://ts.thrustmaster.com/download/pictures/PCMAC/TWCS/TWCS_PC_Generic_Mapping.pdf). Physical buttons 1, 2 and 3 are separate push buttons; 4/5 are the two contacts of one bidirectional switch; 6 is ministick click; 7–14 are contacts of two four-way hats. The separate POV has eight directions. The antenna input is a rotating wheel with a continuous axis, despite the software name “Slider 0.” Five analog axes are named in hardware. Hats, switches and the ministick are on the throttle handle. Marketing button totals are not repeated as physical buttons.
- Airbus quadrant: visually inspected the [manufacturer manual and mapping](https://ts.thrustmaster.com/download/accessories/Manuals/TCA_Quadrant/TCA_Quadrant_Airbus_Edition_user_manual.pdf), printed pages 1–4. Replaced eight supposed standalone push buttons with the physical controls: two lever push buttons, two base push buttons, two engine master switches and an engine-mode selector. The rear engine-pair selector is a separate configuration control. Axis-region events are virtual; reverse-unlock latches do not add separate push buttons.
- SCG Premium: installed button count follows the [European exact Premium grip listing](https://flightsimcontrols.com/product/scg-kosmosima-premium-stand-alone-grip-gladiator/): three standalone buttons, with the supplied C1 button and A1 hat modules treated as alternatives. Conflicting US copy is not silently combined with the European layout.
- MOZA AY210: the [manufacturer support specification](https://support.mozaracing.com/en/support/solutions/articles/70000679586-moza-ay210-yoke-base-support) establishes thirteen panel switches. A new generic switch type retains that count while leaving mechanism, position and push behavior Unknown. These are not automatically classified as thirteen momentary buttons.
- MOZA MHG: the [manufacturer layout description](https://uk.mozaracing.com/products/mhg-flightstick) establishes three management switches described as five-way. They are retained as generic switches with no guessed direction or center-click mapping, rather than three-position toggles. The software-selectable Hall stick is counted once.
- Pedal axes: schema now distinguishes rudder and toe-brake axes from hand-operated levers and electrical rockers. Product evidence remains attached to each control. Explicitly documented rudder axes are included even where older notes said the schema could not represent them.
- VKB K-switches: documented center presses remain attached to their parent controls through the new toggle push field. They are not added again to standalone button totals.
- Turtle Beach: lever-end detent contacts stay attached to their physical levers. Unverified Xbox navigation mappings are not duplicated as additional physical button groups. The [Flight system support guide](https://support.turtlebeach.com/articles/en_US/Knowledge/4409140258715-VelocityOne-Flight-Features-And-Controls) describes yoke pitch/roll and throttle/quadrant movements; unrelated toe-brake and twist axis names were removed from the yoke system. Remaining console mapping and trigger encoding questions keep coverage partial.

## Representation rules

Unknown hat directions, toggle positions, lever analog behavior and pressability remain absent in source data and display as Unknown. Generic switches preserve manufacturer-documented physical counts without inventing mechanisms or bindings. Hardware axis lists and axis controls describe the same inputs; derived totals use the greater verified inventory/list count rather than adding duplicate representations. Spare modules, detent profiles and firmware mappings are excluded from installed physical counts. Complete coverage describes the physical inventory; optional properties may still be unconfirmed.

Research notes describing missing schema capabilities can predate these changes. The current schema supports generic switches, arbitrary selector position counts, rotary/lever/toggle center pushes, dedicated axes, and optional unknown mechanics.

## Final WINCTRL rendered-page pass

The [rendered-page follow-up](controls-winctrl-browser-followup.md) recovered ICP controls, the F-16 throttle revision, exact-handed Ursa Minor manuals and pedal axis evidence. Five-way marketing names remain in labels/notes without claiming five directional contacts or an undocumented center click. Pressable throttle wheels retain Unknown rotation mode unless the source establishes analog versus incremental behavior. Folding/locking hardware is excluded from electrical switch counts while its input behavior remains unresolved. The Orion base, F/A-18 throttle handles and ambiguous PFP3 identity remain partial; empty inventories do not assert absence.
