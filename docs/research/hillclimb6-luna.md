# WINCTRL catalog gaps — hillclimb 6 (Luna)

Checked 2026-10-20. Scope: WINCTRL component records only, with emphasis on physical control inventories, platform evidence and regional prices.

## Method and sources

Queried WINCTRL's official Download Center at `POST https://eu.winctrl.com/home/download/selectProductDownload` with `typeId: 4` and the assigned goods IDs. The response exposed official manual names and IPFS hashes, including HUD1, KA-50 collective, ViperAce EX throttle, EFIS L/R, FCU1, MFD1, ICP, PCR, PTO2 and the ViperAce grip manuals. The endpoint returned a common manual list rather than rows keyed to the submitted goods IDs; model-to-manual identity was cross-checked against the official goods details and the existing official manual URLs in the records.

Used the exact official EU goods-detail records and the official EU / US catalog API snapshots for product descriptions and listed prices. Product detail pages are readable through the API even when their normal web pages time out. Current EU and US listed prices matched the existing entries where present. The catalog endpoints did not provide a UK listing; no currency conversion was used.

- No UK price appeared in the official EU and US catalog API responses. UK fields remain unset rather than converted from other currencies.
- KA-50 press-button totals conflict within the official product description; EFIS selector counts and remaining keys are not itemized; FCU buttons are not counted; PCR function labels do not fully resolve physical control count; PTO2 does not subdivide its 41 switches.
- No control counts were inferred from key-output numbers in diagrams.
