## Remaining unknowns and schema caveats

The Viper mapping labels discrete inputs but the diagram's geometry does not reliably distinguish every switch, trigger, and push control subtype or behavior; those details remain partial rather than inferred. Official calibration instructions confirm the Viper rotary axes and center detent positions, but do not state the number of tactile intermediate stops. The HOTAS schema has no representation for passive mechanical friction adjusters, so the Warthog friction wheel is documented in evidence notes and omitted from electrical control inventory. Toggle groups with mixed momentary/latching positions are represented with descriptive labels because the schema cannot encode behavior per position.
