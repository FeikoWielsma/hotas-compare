## Schema notes

Current schema permits unknown hat directions and toggle positions (omit optional fields), unknown lever analog behavior, rotary mode/push/positions and lever push. No schema extension is required for these unknowns. Genuine gaps remain: pedal-axis-specific input, passive mechanical adjustments, and precise per-position momentary/latching behavior within grouped toggle entries. Those are explained in product notes rather than represented as electrical inputs.
