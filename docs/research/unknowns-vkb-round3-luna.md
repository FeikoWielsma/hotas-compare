### Physical controls and connection corrections

- `stecs-throttle-standard`: corrected the MTG-R center-push 4-way hat count from 3 to 1; corrected installed grip buttons from 4 to 5 (2 on MTG-L and 3 on MTG-R). The [Standard Mk.II listing](https://flightsimcontrols.com/product/stecs-throttle-system-standard-mk-ii/) says one installed 4-way hat. The three 5-way hat modules are spare/swappable modules, not three additional installed hats.
- `stecs-throttle-max`: corrected MTG-R center-push 4-way hats from 3 to 2; corrected installed grip buttons from 4 to 5. The [Max Mk.II listing](https://flightsimcontrols.com/product/stecs-throttle-system-max-mk-ii/) says two installed 4-way hats; its three 5-way hats are replacement modules.
- `f14-combat-grip`: label now follows the manufacturer wording “four-stage ammo selector.” The source does not explain whether the safety lock is a separate input; existing four-position selector representation and push are retained.
- `sem-side-extension-module`: changed sensor to unknown and USB connectivity to unknown/false. The exact [US SEM page](https://vkbsimcontrollers.com/products/gnx-sem-side-extension-module-us) says the item ships with the proprietary GNX BUS interface and “a USB connection is not included”; direct PC USB requires separately installing a GNX USB Controller (HID-Main). USB Type-C shown on the SEM is its BUS connector, not evidence of a USB port. Its MaRS sensor type is not stated on the exact SEM listing.
- `t-rudder-mk5`: the Mk.V listing confirms a reversible hard/soft-center cam, not swappable cams; retained its adjustable-center setting and cleared the incorrect `cams_swappable: true` claim.

VKB listings describe programmable red/RGB LEDs on SCG and indicator LEDs on bases/modules. These are not evidence that the buttons themselves are backlit, so no button `backlit` fields were inferred from LED counts.

## SCG Premium standalone weight check

The identity is now established: the SCG file is the **Premium Gladiator/NXT/EVO grip-only SKU**, with the base explicitly excluded. The [exact EU product page](https://flightsimcontrols.com/product/scg-kosmosima-premium-stand-alone-grip-gladiator/) supports NXT/NXT EVO compatibility, differentiating it from the separate [Gunfighter SCG grip page](https://flightsimcontrols.com/product/scg-kosmosima-premium-right-handed/). Both pages list 550 g and include a palm rest plus spare C1/A1 modules, trigger cover, screwdriver and screws. Neither page says whether 550 g is bare grip, full included package, or shipping mass. The prior `380 g` had no exact source attached in the YAML. I removed that unsupported scalar and left the physical grip mass unresolved; it should not be reported as a confirmed 550 g net grip weight.

### Remaining control-level unknowns

- **F-14 GNX:** the exact [Gladiator grip page](https://flightsimcontrols.com/product/f-14-stand-alone-grip-for-gladiator/) gives a center-push hat but no direction count. It calls the ammo control a four-stage selector, but does not say whether the safety lock is another input. It names the Emergency Autopilot Disable trigger separately without giving its stage count.
- **SCG Premium GNX:** the exact [standalone grip page](https://flightsimcontrols.com/product/scg-kosmosima-premium-stand-alone-grip-gladiator/) confirms three 4-way center-push hats and an 8-way center-push analog ministick. The secondary rapid-fire trigger's stages are unspecified. The page names programmable red/RGB LEDs but does not say the buttons themselves are backlit.
- **KG12 GNX:** the exact [GNX add-on page](https://vkbsimcontrollers.com/products/gnx-kg12-add-on-grip-us) identifies the physical controls but not folding-trigger stages or a center push on the 8-way hat. It does not enumerate button backlighting.
- **MCG Ultimate:** the exact [Ultimate page](https://flightsimcontrols.com/product/modern-combat-grip-ultimate-edition/) gives 5 analog axes without twist or 6 with the optional twist, plus a selectable ministick or hat module. It does not specify the optional hat's direction count or push; the 24–32 figure is programmable/logical button count, not a physical button count. Per-axis sensor models are not enumerated.
- **STECS Standard/Max:** the exact [Standard](https://flightsimcontrols.com/product/stecs-throttle-system-standard-mk-ii/) and [Max](https://flightsimcontrols.com/product/stecs-throttle-system-max-mk-ii/) pages confirm installed control types and counts, but do not give trigger stage counts or the MTG-L wheel-encoder push behavior. K-switch position counts and momentary/maintained action are not stated. The included spare hat/button modules are not physical installed controls.
- **STECS Mini:** the exact [Mini page](https://flightsimcontrols.com/product/stecs-throttle-system-mini-mk-ii/) gives two base analog axes and one grip ministick plus one analog wheel axis, but does not name both physical mechanisms mapped to the base axes; the axis inventory was not expanded with inferred axis names.
- **SEM:** exact [EU](https://flightsimcontrols.com/product/gnx-sem-side-extension-module/) and [US](https://vkbsimcontrollers.com/products/gnx-sem-side-extension-module-us) pages give one rotary switch, two multifunction axes and two K-switches with center push, but not rotary/K-switch position counts, momentary behavior, or axis names/functions. The exact pages do not establish the module's full material composition or sensor type.
