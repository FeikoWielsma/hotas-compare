# SOL-R catalog refresh

Checked 2026-10-20 against the official Dutch Thrustmaster product pages. Prices are manufacturer-listed EUR prices for Netherlands, including VAT, not currency conversions or confirmed stock. All six products list PC; console support is not advertised. Downloaded images are first-party manufacturer assets.

- [Sol-R 6 Throttle](https://www.thrustmaster.com/nl-nl/products/sol-r-6-throttle/): €99.99; the Netherlands retailer panel shows no resellers, so stock is unconfirmed. Documents an 80 mm sliding throttle, four configurable cam settings, four buttons, two 4-way hats, 8-way POV, thumbwheel, ministick and self-centering rudder. Wheel reporting and push actions remain unspecified. 670 g, 170 × 205 × 125 mm, USB-C, T.A.R.G.E.T.
- [Sol-R 5 Base](https://www.thrustmaster.com/nl-nl/products/sol-r-5-base/): €129.99. Two 16-bit Hall axes plus mini-throttle axis. Four toggles, eight pads, selector and rotary knob. Switch positions and knob reporting are unspecified; 23 programmable actions are not 23 physical buttons. 960 g, 196 × 196 × 67 mm, USB-C.
- [Sol-R 3 AVA Add-On Grip](https://www.thrustmaster.com/nl-nl/products/sol-r-3-ava-add-on-grip/): €99.99. Ambidextrous, two triggers (one dual-position), two hats, ministick, thumbwheel and Hall Z-axis. Requires AVA/Sol-R base; legacy Warthog requires converter, disables Z and wheel axis, maps ministick to POV without click and restricts lighting. Includes STAR cams for AVA. 309 g, 75 × 115 × 200 mm.
- [Sol-R 4 HOTAS](https://www.thrustmaster.com/nl-nl/products/sol-r-4-hotas/): €279.99. One Sol-R stick/base and sliding throttle; modeled using Sol-R 1 and Sol-R 6 component references. The page documents the shared base inventory: four toggles, eight pads, mini-throttle, selector and rotary knob. Programmable actions are not expanded into invented physical controls.
- [Sol-R 2 HOSAS Space Sim Duo](https://www.thrustmaster.com/nl-nl/products/sol-r-2-hosas-space-sim-duo/): €329.99. Two reconfigurable Sol-R sticks, represented by two references to the same ambidextrous component. Manufacturer reports six main axes plus ten virtual axes across the pair. Repeated references preserve quantity and derived totals.
- [Sol-R 1 Flightstick](https://www.thrustmaster.com/nl-nl/products/sol-r-1-flightstick/): €179.99. Corrected the old generic name/right-only handedness. Manufacturer lists three 16-bit main axes plus five virtual axes; `axis_count: 8` preserves that published total without inventing the unresolved virtual mapping. 1270 g, 196 × 196 × 247 mm. Existing UK/US prices remain historical, with their original check dates.

Unknown hat directions, pushes and rotary behavior remain unspecified. Control evidence is partial. Platform support for a bundle is recorded on its own source rather than inferred from its members.
