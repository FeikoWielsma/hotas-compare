# WINCTRL catalog gaps — hillclimb 6c (luna3)

Checked 2026-10-21. Scope: exact regional shop prices, selected WINCTRL hardware fields, partially documented inventories, and bundle consistency.

## Method

Queried the official EU and US catalog APIs (`POST https://eu.winctrl.com/home/list/get` and `POST https://us.winwingsim.com/home/list/get`, `{"all":1}`) and checked listed records by exact ID, title, goods number, and live price. Exact regional product pages are linked below. The WINCTRL Download Center API was queried with `typeId: 4` and `list: [{sku_id: 0, shop_id: ...}]`; as in round 6, it returned the current common manual list rather than a product-keyed mapping. Manual identity was matched against official goods numbers and existing product pages. Rendered manual diagrams and axis calibration pages were inspected for the listed Orion 2 and URSA MINOR models.
