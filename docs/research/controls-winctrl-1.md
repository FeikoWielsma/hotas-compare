# WinCtrl physical controls research

> Research trail: consult [the integration review](controls-integration-review.md) and [current catalog audit](../control-data-audit.md) for subsequent corrections and current schema capabilities.

Checked 2026-10-19. Controls inventories and evidence coverage were reviewed for the assigned WinCtrl products. Only the Cyber Taurus base reached complete coverage from an exact primary manual. Other storefront detail pages were fetched, but their readable response contained only client-side placeholders. Where exact physical counts could not be verified, the controls array is left empty rather than repeating unsupported numbers; partial means the inventory is undocumented, not that the product has no controls.

## Data and schema notes

- `controls_evidence.coverage` is `complete` only for the Cyber Taurus base; the other ten items are `partial` because exact physical inventory evidence could not be read for those variants.
- The Cyber Taurus manual's physical `Power Button` can be represented as a `button`, but the schema has no distinct power-control type. Its emergency stop is a cable/interface port and is not an input control.
- Empty arrays on partial entries mean no verified inventory was available, not confirmed physical absence. No alternative modules were counted as simultaneously installed.
