# WINCTRL hillclimb 3 — product sweep and control follow-up

Checked 2026-10-20. Scope: `src/content/components/winctrl/*.yaml`, `src/content/bundles/winctrl/*.yaml`, `public/images/winctrl/`, and this note.

## Sources and method

The official EU store product catalog was fetched from [WINCTRL `/home/list/get`](https://eu.winctrl.com/home/list/get). It returned 153 listing rows across HOTAS, HOSAS, Joystick, Throttle, Rudder, Combo, Panel, Base, Grip, accessories and spare-parts categories. Product records were checked against the existing WINCTRL YAML inventory; all distinct product records found in this sweep were already cataloged except the products and exact combos added in this pass. The separate official download-center response was queried with `POST https://eu.winctrl.com/home/download/selectProductDownload`, JSON body `{"typeId":4,"list":[{"sku_id":0,"shop_id":<goods id>}]}`. The PDF manuals were downloaded from WINCTRL's official IPFS CDN and rendered with PyMuPDF for visual inspection. Only official first-party store photos were kept in the new catalog image files.

The WinCtrl store sometimes lists configurable or multi-item products with misleading titles or repeated item codes. Listing description / goods code and default contents were used for identity. No inferred console support was added; PC is supported where the manufacturer lists PC compatibility.

## Existing inventory details established

- Panel of Auto Pilot 3 store specification for goods 1065: 17 pushbuttons, five 360° rotary knobs, one separate five-position 120° rotary knob, a rotary encoder wheel, and a two-position toggle. Rotary electrical behavior beyond those stated mechanics is not inferred. [Official listing](https://eu.winctrl.com/mobileview/goods-details.html?id=1065).
- MFD1 controls from the official Legend MIP product specification: 4 seesaw buttons, 20 square buttons, 16 rectangular buttons, one three-position slide knob and one encoder. Axis totals and encoder push behavior are not stated. [Official listing](https://eu.winctrl.com/mobileview/goods-details.html?id=549).
- UFC1 specification lists a backlight switch, five backlight function selectors, 12 numeric keypad keys, three-position ADF toggle, two VOL potentiometer axes and one BRT potentiometer axis (three analog axes total). The 18 button-key count treats the five function selectors as tactile selector buttons per the store's named control layout. [Official listing](https://eu.winctrl.com/view/goods-details.html?id=378).
- Exact L-hand Ursa manuals were rendered and inspected: the Airline L multifunction HAT is four-way and pressable; Fighter L and Space L have four-way HATs without documented center press, and their triggers visibly have first/second stages. The diagrams' output/key numbers were not used to infer total physical control counts. [Airline L manual](https://eu.winctrl.com/static/download/QmTNG9wYC7V5h4a5U4m2ZbYY55uag2c3wQc2MUw9m7KXHK?filename=URSA+MINOR-Airlines-Joystick+L-User+Manual-V1.3.pdf), [Fighter L manual](https://eu.winctrl.com/static/download/QmabSXmvPxNp2ZXHidaDhGq73XGHyKicn6YWrgQksanXDp?filename=URSA-MINOR-Fighter-Joystick-L-User-Manual-v1.3.pdf), [Space L manual](https://eu.winctrl.com/static/download/QmfFcdFqWFoe3S1vo51wpCPW9ZKRnDc6mRPb7sd6S45y6R?filename=URSA-MINOR-Space-Joystick-L-User-Manual-V1.3.pdf).

## Still unset

- PFP-3N, PFP-4 and PFP-7 each have 71 physically drawn pushbutton caps in the official button-layout diagrams; MCDU has 74. The installed caps were counted visually rather than inferred from an aircraft standard or from their output labels.
- HUD1's V1.2 manual diagram shows four distinct 0–4095 rotary axis ranges; these are recorded as four axes. The manual does not give a written button inventory, so buttons remain unset. UFC1 and MFD1 inventory rows have been improved from the official store pages as above.
- ViperAce EX throttle grip button positions remain unclear: the official diagram is an unnumbered button-position illustration, and current listing/manual sources don't give a count tied to the exact EX throttle grip SKU. Its inventory remains partial.
- URSA MINOR 32 Throttle Metal manual confirms separate RX and RY axes and the two throttle handles; both handles and axes are represented. Manual key-position drawing visually identifies two base FIRE/FAULT pushbuttons, counted at 0.3 mm travel. Any short-travel group without identifiable physical keys remains unset.
- The exact installed 3X0 alternate configuration is not specified; both removable options are represented as alternatives. KA-50 grip switch/hat tally still lacks an official written mapping. Its verified axes and the 3X0 partial inventory carry explicit reasons for omitted details.

Source and current SKU claims are captured in each YAML `sources` / `controls_evidence` field. Weights, dimensions and prices were recorded only when useful for a complete source-backed record and are not used as substitutes for control inventory.
