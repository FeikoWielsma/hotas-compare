# Thrustmaster catalog refresh

Checked 2026-10-20. The assigned official Dutch Thrustmaster product pages were checked for compatibility, controls, hardware, dimensions, connector details and region-specific prices. Product-specific Dutch EUR prices are recorded only when the page lists a price for that product. Prices shown for recommended accessories/configurations are excluded.

## TCA SKY YOKE (XBOX / PC)

Current product page explicitly identifies Xbox, PC, iOS and Android compatibility, 24 action buttons, mini-stick, 8-way hat, two analog triggers, 7 axes with 16-bit H.E.A.R.T., 9 cm pitch travel and 180-degree rotation. The 24 action-button marketing total is documented without treating it as physical button count. The page did not expose a Dutch EUR price. Listing image is locally referenced.

## T.FLIGHT HOTAS NEO ACE COMBAT 8 editions

Recorded as two distinct current SKUs: PlayStation / PC and Xbox / PC. The PS page is published on the official Ireland locale (the supplied Dutch slug was not accessible); the official Dutch Xbox page was accessible. Both list 7 axes, 16 action buttons, mini-stick, rapid-fire trigger, hat switch, High-G Turns button and detachable throttle. Their 16-action marketing total is not counted as physical buttons. Both are 16-bit; H.E.A.R.T. is specified on the Z axis. Dimensions are 350 × 200 × 230 mm, weight 1,425 g, USB-C. Dutch prices are €129.99 each. PS edition compatibility and vibration are limited to PS5/PS4/PC (vibration PS5 and PC); Xbox edition supports Xbox Series X|S, Xbox One and PC (vibration Xbox Series X|S and PC).

## HOTAS WARTHOG MKII and base

The system and standalone base pages are current and list PC. System page lists 320 × 260 × 440 mm, 6,560 g and €549.99. The standalone base is 110 × 110 × 94 mm, 996 g, uses H.E.A.R.T. and a 6-pin grip connector (up to 128 buttons and 8 axes); Dutch price €99.99. Bundle references the standalone MKII base plus existing Warthog grip and throttle components.

## AIRBUS Add-On Grip

Current left-hand grip, compatible with AVA, Warthog MKII and Sol-R bases. Official page specifies 5 buttons, 4 axes including Z, a dual-mode trigger, dimensions 78 × 83 × 195 mm and weight 203 g. Platform compatibility follows the connected base. No product-specific Dutch EUR price was displayed.

## OMNI Extension

Current mechanical extension accessory for AVA and Sol-R bases. Reinforced polyamide construction. Its 6-DoF language describes the configured stick system, not electronic axes in this accessory; standalone USB is false. Nearby prices are for recommended products and are not assigned to the OMNI Extension. No product-specific Dutch EUR price was displayed.

## T.Flight Hotas 5 Microsoft Flight Simulator Edition

Current integrated HOTAS. Manufacturer lists Windows 10/11 and PS5/PS4, not Xbox. Page specifies 5 axes, 14 buttons, one trigger and one hat, detachable throttle, dual rudder via twist axis or throttle tilt, dimensions 350 × 200 × 235 mm and weight 2,060 g. The 14-button promotional total is not modeled as a physical button count. Dutch price €99.99.

## HOTAS WARTHOG AVA EDITION

Current bundle page identifies the licensed Warthog grip, dual throttle and AVA base; catalog references those existing component records rather than duplicating them. Manufacturer lists PC. Dimensions 157/231 × 272 × 198/305 mm, weight 7,751 g, USB-C. Dutch price €599.99.
