# MOZA catalog unknowns — round 3 (Haiku)

Checked 2026-10-20. Scope: the 16 MOZA component records and the two MOZA bundle records. Each component was checked against its official product page and, where one exists, its official support article or manual. Retailer listings were not used for values. Other brands were not touched.

Method: one fetch per exact official page, with prompts asking for quoted wording on platform, controls, dimensions, weight, materials, connectivity, software, and throw angle. Where a category was not stated, it was left unset rather than inferred.

Platform rule applied: console support is `unsupported` only when the official page names an explicit exclusion ("PC only", or "does not support Xbox or PlayStation"). A "PC" spec line alone is not an exclusion, so Xbox and PS5 stay `unknown`.

Schema note: `standalone_usb` defaults to `true` in the schema and cannot be left unset. Where the page is silent, the value shown below is a schema-forced default and is flagged, not a sourced fact.

## Purchased variant details

- **MH16 (grip):** The official "In the Box" list is "MH16 Flightstick, toolkit". The AB9 base, the Z-axis module, and the side expansion are not in the box. The side expansion is not stated as sold separately or included, so its controls (5-way hat + 3-position selector) stay excluded.
- **AB9 (base):** Sold separately. The box is "AB9 FFB Base, data cord, wrench, user manual".
- **AB9 + MH16 combo bundle:** No official combo SKU was found on any MOZA store. The AB9 and MH16 are sold separately (US: AB9 sale $499, MH16 sale $149). The bundle record keeps `prices: []`, which is correct.
- **AY210 bundle:** The official handle is the MFY Yoke, not J04. The bundle is AY210 base + MFY, which the bundle record now reflects.
- **AB6 bundle (not in catalog):** The official AB6 bundle is AB6 base + MHG grip (EUR 449, sale). The AB6 bundle page's "MHG" dimensions and weight (142.5 × 202 × 313 mm, 395 g) match the MFY yoke, not the MHG, so they were not used.

## Conflicts recorded (not resolved)

- **MH16 inputs:** product page says 30, overview says 27. Neither is treated as a physical-control count.
- **MTQ controls:** official page says "23 physical controls", but itemized counts total 31. Itemized counts recorded as stated.
- **Z-axis sensor:** "16-bit Hall" (description and FAQ) vs "15-bit (32768 PPR)" (spec table). 16-bit kept, conflict noted.
- **MHG inputs:** UK page lists a "hat switch" and no mini stick; manual names a mini stick. Mini stick kept.
- **AB6 bundle page:** MHG dimensions/weight duplicate MFY values. Not used.

## Remaining unknowns (source limits)

- **AB9 throw angle:** "Maximum Limit Angle: ±20°" with no per-side or total wording. `throw_deg` unset.
- **Z-axis and AB6 throw angles:** same situation. Unset.
- **MRP pedals toe-brake axes:** "differential braking" does not say whether there are one or two toe-brake axes. Existing two-axis representation is retained and flagged.
- **TQA/TQB grip input count:** no official grip count is given. The existing two-throttle-grip entry is unverified and still flagged.
- **MTP detent count:** the spec gives "adjustable afterburner, idle lockout, center detents" with no count. The existing count of 2 is retained unverified.
- **MH16 weight, MHG/MA3X/Z-axis/TQA/TQB dimensions:** not stated on the official pages.
- **AY210 and AY90 sensor/encoder resolution:** the official support pages state "15-bit"/"magnetic encoder" in places, but this round did not re-verify the 15-bit figure on the AY210/AY90 pages. Existing values unchanged.
- **J04 record:** no official J04 product exists in the sources checked. The record is now orphaned by the bundle fix. Parent decides whether to keep or remove it.
- **`standalone_usb` on MHG:** page is silent. Schema default `true` remains. Not sourced.
- **Console support on MA3X, MHG, AY90, MFY, J04, Z-axis, TQA, TQB, MRP damper, MRP pedals, MH16:** no official exclusion wording found, so these stay `unknown`. Explicit exclusion wording exists for AB9, AY210, MTP, and MTQ; see the table. Only explicit "PC only" or "does not support Xbox or PlayStation" wording was used to mark `unsupported`.
