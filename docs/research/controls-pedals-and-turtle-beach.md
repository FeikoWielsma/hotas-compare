# Controls research: pedals and Turtle Beach

> Research trail: consult [the integration review](controls-integration-review.md) and [current catalog audit](../control-data-audit.md) for subsequent corrections and current schema capabilities.

Checked 2026-10-19. Counts below describe installed physical inputs, not firmware mappings or marketing totals. `controls` cannot express a rudder axis (or arbitrary pedal axis), so pedal YAML records brake pedals as analog levers and documents the additional rudder axis in `controls_evidence.notes`.

## MFG

- [Crosswind Cessna Footrests](../../src/content/components/mfg/crosswind-cessna-footrests.yaml) — complete inventory: this is the Cessna-style footrest accessory, with no electrical controls. It mounts to the Crosswind parent pedal assembly; the footrests are not a separate pedal controller. [MFG accessory listing](https://mfg-sim.com/rudder-acessories/footrest-kit-for-mfg-rudder-pedals.html)
- [Crosswind ME-109 Combat Footrests](../../src/content/components/mfg/crosswind-combat-footrests.yaml) — complete: the Combat/Universal footrest option is a mechanical footrest pair, with no switches or electrical inputs. [MFG accessory listing](https://mfg-sim.com/rudder-acessories/footrest-kit-for-mfg-rudder-pedals.html)
- [Crosswind Hydraulic Damper](../../src/content/components/mfg/crosswind-hydraulic-damper.yaml) — complete: the adjustable damper is a mechanical add-on for V1–V3 pedals and adds resistance, not electrical controls. The V3 product page lists it as a separate optional accessory. [MFG damper listing](https://mfg-sim.com/rudder-acessories/hydraulic-damper-universal-kit-for-v1-3-pedals.html), [MFG V3 base/accessories](https://mfg-sim.com/rudders/mfg-crosswind-v3-base.html)
- [Crosswind V3 Graphite Rudder Pedals](../../src/content/components/mfg/crosswind-v3-graphite-pedals.yaml) — complete: two independent analog toe-brake axes, plus one analog rudder axis. MFG identifies independent left/right brake axes and 13-bit HID hardware. No physical push buttons or switches are identified. This record is the Graphite/Bf109 package; alternate footrests alter pedal geometry, not the base input set. [MFG V3 specifications and brakes](https://mfg-sim.com/rudders/mfg-crosswind-v3-base.html), [Bf109 product variant](https://mfg-sim.com/rudders/mfg-crosswind-v3-me109-rudder-pedals.html)

## Slaw Device

- [RH Rotor V2 Pedals](../../src/content/components/slaw/rotor-pedals.yaml) — complete: manufacturer specifies three axes for rudder and two separate toe brakes, with 13-bit non-contact sensors. No discrete buttons/switches are listed. Optional damper is not included in the pedal package. [RH Rotor V2 product and specs](https://slawdevice.com/en/rudder-pedals/48-pre-order-rh-rotor-v2-rudder-pedals.html)
- [Slaw Device Damper Kit](../../src/content/components/slaw/slaw-damper-kit.yaml) — complete as a mechanical accessory inventory: it has no discrete controls. The RH Rotor page confirms the hydraulic damper is optional and not included. I did not find a standalone first-party damper-kit page, so exact damper-kit SKU/compatibility remains unverified. [RH Rotor V2 product page](https://slawdevice.com/en/rudder-pedals/48-pre-order-rh-rotor-v2-rudder-pedals.html)
- [RX Viper V2 Pedals](../../src/content/components/slaw/viper-v2-rudder-pedals.yaml) — partial: V2-era product-specific documentation is sparse. Slaw states V2 production was suspended indefinitely in 2022; its current V3 product documentation says assembly is similar to V2 and describes three axes (rudder + two toe brakes), but that does not prove every V2 detail. The YAML records the two analog brakes and explicitly leaves full V2 confirmation unresolved. [V2 discontinuation notice](https://slawdevice.com/en/blog/post/7-Announcement-RX-Viper-V2-rudder-pedals.html), [V3 page identifying V2 as previous generation and listing axis count](https://slawdevice.com/en/glowna/47-pre-order-rx-viper-v3-rudder-pedals-silver.html), [manufacturer downloads covering old RX Viper/V2 calibration software](https://slawdevice.com/en/content/7-downloads)

## Turtle Beach

- [VelocityOne Flight Yoke System](../../src/content/components/turtle-beach/velocityone-flight-yoke-system.yaml) — partial: the support guide names two POVs, two hats, two triggers, B1/B2, LB/RB, ten quadrant buttons (B3–B12), four quadrant detent buttons, four TPM levers (one separate throttle plus the three TPM linear levers; the YAML groups four rotational quadrant levers and three TPM levers), Xbox controls, four FMD buttons, and a trim wheel. The published “18 assignable buttons” marketing count does not reconcile cleanly with the detailed physical nomenclature, so it was not used as a control count. Trigger behavior, diagonal POV gating, and trim-wheel encoding remain unspecified. [Features and controls](https://support.turtlebeach.com/articles/en_US/Knowledge/4409140258715-VelocityOne-Flight-Features-And-Controls), [detent-button firmware note](https://support.turtlebeach.com/articles/en_US/Knowledge/4416914225947-VelocityOne-Flight-Detent-Buttons), [official product page](https://www.turtlebeach.com/products/velocity-one-flight)
- [VelocityOne Flightstick](../../src/content/components/turtle-beach/velocityone-flightstick.yaml) — partial: the handbook names one digital 8-way POV, one analog POV with push, one trigger, two multifunction levers with two detent buttons, B1–B8, microswitches B16/B17, touchpad click, FMD configurator wheel (right-select/left-back), and trim wheel. Xbox navigation controls are also present, but their physical switch count is not made clear in the control diagram. The trim wheel can be set as analog or directional digital input. Trigger stages and multifunction-lever input mode are not specified. [Pilot’s operating handbook](https://cdn.turtlebeach.com/device/qig/velocityone--flightstick/velocityone--flightstick_367-6703-v1.pdf), [official product page](https://uk.turtlebeach.com/products/velocity-one-flight-stick)
- [VelocityOne Rudder Pedals](../../src/content/components/turtle-beach/velocityone-rudder-pedals.yaml) — complete: three 10-bit Hall-effect axes (one return-to-center rudder axis and two spring-loaded toe-brake axes). The included pedal shapes are alternatives, not additional installed controls. No discrete buttons/switches are documented. [Technical specifications](https://www.turtlebeach.com/products/velocity-one-rudder), [quick flight guide and axis mapping](https://cdn.turtlebeach.com/device/additional-files/velocityone--rudder/velocityone--rudder_363-1099-v1.pdf)

## Schema limitations and unresolved details

`ControlItemSchema` has no generic analog-axis control. Accordingly the rudder axes are explained in evidence notes and hardware axes rather than represented as a lever/button. It also has no explicit dual-mode wheel (analog axis versus directional digital control) or distinct back/forward wheel clicks. Turtle Beach materials leave exact yoke trigger behavior, yoke POV diagonal behavior, Flightstick trigger stages, multifunction-lever mode, and several integrated Xbox button counts unspecified. Those details remain unknown rather than inferred.
